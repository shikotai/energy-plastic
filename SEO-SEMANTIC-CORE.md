## On-page rules

- One clear H1 per page.
- Use the primary page theme naturally in the H1, intro and at least one H2 where appropriate.
- Do not repeat keyword phrases unnaturally.
- Keep titles descriptive and unique.
- Keep descriptions concise and specific to the page.
- Add new public news URLs to the sitemap when separate news article routes are introduced.
