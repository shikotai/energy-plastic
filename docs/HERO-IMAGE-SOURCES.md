# Hero image sources

The hero photos are from Pexels and were shown as **Free to use** on their source pages when selected on 2026-08-17.
Since 2026-11-20 they are stored locally in `src/assets/hero/` (WebP, 1400×950, quality 80) instead of being loaded from images.pexels.com.
